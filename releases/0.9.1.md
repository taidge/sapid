# Sapid 0.9.1

NativeSelect supports decorative dividers with Glory 0.9.1. OverflowList
updates item visibility as available space changes. The widget showcase
revises its interactive examples and configurators, including Timeline and
ThemeIcon.

On native hosts, the AreaChart, BarChart, LineChart and CompositeChart
configurators use the available preview width. The formatting toolbar's
alignment controls retain square hit targets.

In WebKit, the SegmentedControl selection indicator animates between the
measured bounds of the selected labels and honors reduced motion. Styled
single-row NativeSelect controls retain their configured height and the
platform picker.
